# /beep

> Source: https://manual.mikrotik.com/docs/cli-reference/beep

Type: Command

Beep the built-in speaker. Supported only on devices with a beeper. Check device pages at [mikrotik.com](https://mikrotik.com)

**Arguments**

- `frequency` (num): Sound frequency in Hz
- `length` (time): Duration of the sound in seconds

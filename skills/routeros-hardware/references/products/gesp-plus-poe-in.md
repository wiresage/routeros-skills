# GESP+POE-IN

> Source: https://manual.mikrotik.com/hardware/gesp-plus-poe-in
>
> Category: Accessories

*[Figure]*

GESP+PoE is a passive PoE injector with surge protection. Combine it with the GESP devices for maximum security and peace of mind. See the [GESP+POE-IN product page](https://mikrotik.com/product/gesp_poe_in) for full specifications and brochures.

## Connecting

- Connect to your host device with an rj45 Ethernet connector end.
  *[Figure]*
- Secure grounding to your rackmount or other preferable grounded source.
- Use an Ethernet cable to connect with the device on the mast to deliver PoE.
- Apply power using the power adapter.

## Mounting

It is possible to attach the device to a wall or mast using the provided bracket:

*[Figure]*

- Guide screw through a bracket and secure it to the wall, use dowel depending on the wall material.
- Clip the device to the bracket.
  Please connect the grounding cable to the well-grounded source.

## LED status

The device has an LED indicating the active status of the connected Ethernet port.

>
> Only on help.mikrotik.com: the manual has no equivalent page. The help site is frozen; this page was last edited 2019-11-05.

This describes how to register in the MikroTik support portal.

## Step-by-step guide

The new MikroTik support portal allows you to see a history of your support requests. To access it, you have two options:

1. If you already have emailed support, and received a response with "View this issue" link, you must click on "Forgot your password" link in the support portal login page.
2. If you have never emailed support, you can click "Sign up for an account" to create your account and make support requests.
